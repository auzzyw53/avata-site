Avata

Avata develops treatments for autoimmune disease using wild hookworm chemistries.

Bioprospecting the parasites of wild primates to find immune-regulating chemistries for autoimmune therapeutics.

01

Discover

We collect parasites from wild primates in the field and apply cutting-edge analytical methods to understand the chemistries they secrete.

02

Develop

Promising molecules are synthesized and tested for immune effects, then refined into drug candidates.

03

Treat

The goal is oral medicines for autoimmune disease, starting with patients who have run out of first-line options.
